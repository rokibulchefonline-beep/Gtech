Important Package Notes, Terms & Scope Guidelines

  • Setup Fee: A one-off setup fee of £295 is applicable across all packages for baseline technical auditing, tool configuration, and tag tracking installation.
  • Billing Terms: All packages are billed monthly with zero long-term lock-in contracts. All quoted prices are subject to UK VAT.
  • Article / Blog Content: Core articles and industry topics to be provided by or aligned with the client prior to publication.
  • Customer Reviews Management: Reviews from social media and local directories will be collated and sent to the client once a week for action. GTech Digital does not directly respond to or manage internal customer reviews on behalf of the business.
  • Platinum Paid Campaign Allowance: For Platinum package subscribers ONLY; includes £16 worth of ad campaigns per month.
  • Twitter / X Account Verification: Opening a Twitter/X business account requires client phone number SMS verification that must be completed directly by the client.
  • Newsletter Campaigns: Includes broadcast delivery for up to 2,000 subscribers. Lists exceeding 2,000 subscribers are charged at £25 per additional 500 subscribers.
  • Press Releases: Digital PR and editorial press releases are subject to specific client niche requirements and media distribution scope. Additional syndication charges apply.
  • Facebook Campaign Add-On Scope: Facebook Campaign Add-On covers 1 campaign publishing, custom caption copy, bespoke high-converting creative design, and full campaign performance reporting.
