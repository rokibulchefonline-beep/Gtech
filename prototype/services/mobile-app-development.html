Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Mobile App Development

Should we build a Native app or a Cross-Platform app? +

For most commercial and business applications, cross-platform frameworks like React Native or Flutter provide near-native performance, unified codebase efficiency, and 40% lower development costs. For graphics-heavy or Bluetooth hardware apps, native Swift and Kotlin are recommended.

How long does it take to develop and launch a mobile application? +

A standard Minimum Viable Product (MVP) typically takes 8 to 12 weeks from initial wireframing to App Store and Google Play approval. Complex enterprise apps require 14 to 20 weeks.

Do you manage App Store and Google Play submissions and approvals? +

Yes, 100%. We handle the end-to-end deployment process, including Apple App Store review compliance, Google Play Console release management, and ongoing version updates.

Who owns the mobile app source code and intellectual property? +

You retain 100% complete ownership of all source code, Figma design assets, GitHub repositories, API keys, and deployment credentials upon project completion.
