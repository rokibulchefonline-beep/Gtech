Performance Marketing FAQs

Frequently asked questions about our paid acquisition management

What is performance marketing and how does it differ from traditional advertising?

Performance marketing is an outcomes-driven digital media strategy where every pound spent is tracked, attributed, and optimized toward specific commercial KPIs—such as online purchases, qualified leads, and Return On Ad Spend (ROAS). Unlike brand awareness marketing, performance campaigns are continuously calibrated to lower cost-per-acquisition (CPA) and maximize gross profitability.

What ad platforms do you actively manage?

As a certified Google Premier Partner and Meta Business Partner, we manage Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube, Display), Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Advantage+), TikTok Ads (Spark Ads & TikTok Shop), and Microsoft Advertising. All channels are linked via Google Tag Manager Server-Side, Meta CAPI, and GA4 for unified cross-channel attribution.

What is your minimum monthly ad spend recommendation?

To feed machine-learning algorithms sufficient conversion signals for rapid optimization, we typically recommend a minimum ad budget of £1,500 to £3,000 per month across Google and Meta. For enterprise brands spending £10,000 to £100,000+ monthly, our dedicated account directors implement custom multi-tiered bid caps and custom creative rotation.

How does server-side CAPI tracking improve our ROAS?

Browser-based pixel tracking loses between 20% to 40% of conversion signals due to iOS privacy restrictions, ad blockers, and cookie consent barriers. Server-side tracking (Meta CAPI and Google Enhanced Conversions) sends encrypted first-party purchase data directly from your server to the ad platform, allowing algorithms to target high-intent buyers and dramatically reducing wasted spend.

Do we own our ad accounts and creative assets?

Yes, 100%. You retain full administrative ownership and billing rights of all ad accounts, custom pixels, GA4 telemetry, and creative assets produced. GTech Digital operates with total transparency, and you can access your real-time performance data 24/7 via Looker Studio.

How quickly will we see positive commercial returns?

During the initial 14 to 21-day audit and setup sprint, we immediately eliminate obvious budget waste and calibrate tracking. Most clients see significant CPA reductions within the first 30 days, with full algorithmic scaling and target ROAS achieved by month two and three.
