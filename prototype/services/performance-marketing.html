Ask a question

Performance marketing is an outcomes-driven digital media strategy where every pound spent is tracked, attributed, and optimized toward specific commercial KPIs—such as online purchases, qualified leads, and Return On Ad Spend (ROAS). Unlike brand awareness marketing, performance campaigns are continuously calibrated to lower cost-per-acquisition (CPA) and maximize gross profitability.

As a certified Google Premier Partner and Meta Business Partner, we manage Google Ads (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube, Display), Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram Advantage+), TikTok Ads (Spark Ads & TikTok Shop), and Microsoft Advertising. All channels are linked via Google Tag Manager Server-Side, Meta CAPI, and GA4 for unified cross-channel attribution.

To feed machine-learning algorithms sufficient conversion signals for rapid optimization, we typically recommend a minimum ad budget of £1,500 to £3,000 per month across Google and Meta. For enterprise brands spending £10,000 to £100,000+ monthly, our dedicated account directors implement custom multi-tiered bid caps and creative rotation.

Browser-based pixel tracking loses between 20% to 40% of conversion signals due to iOS privacy restrictions, ad blockers, and cookie consent barriers. Server-side tracking (Meta CAPI and Google Enhanced Conversions) sends encrypted first-party purchase data directly from your server to the ad platform, allowing algorithms to target high-intent buyers and dramatically reducing wasted spend.
