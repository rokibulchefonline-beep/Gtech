Overview

About the Project

Townley Communications sells business telephony, connectivity and managed cloud IT to UK SMEs and mid-market organisations. Deal values are high and sales cycles run to months, so paid media has to be judged on pipeline quality rather than raw lead count.

The account arrived with respectable-looking lead volume and no way to tell which of those leads ever became revenue. Our first job was measurement, not media.

The Challenge

What Was Holding Growth Back

  • Leads counted, revenue not tracked

    Every form fill counted as one conversion regardless of value. A £200 handset enquiry and a £40,000 managed-services contract were optimised toward identically.

  • Broad match draining budget on consumer intent

    Terms like "phone deals" and "broadband" pulled high volumes of consumer traffic into a strictly B2B account, inflating spend against leads that could never close.

  • One landing page for every campaign

    All traffic hit a generic services page. Message-match was poor across a product range spanning VoIP, leased lines and Microsoft 365 migration.

  • No visibility of offline closes

    Deals closed over the phone and in CRM never returned to Google Ads, so smart bidding was optimising against a partial and misleading signal.

The Solution

How We Solved It

  1. Rebuilt tracking around pipeline value

    Implemented value-weighted conversions with offline conversion import from CRM, so bidding optimised toward qualified pipeline rather than form submissions.

    GA4Offline ImportHubSpot

  2. Restructured the account by commercial intent

    Split campaigns by product line and buying stage, moved to tightly themed exact and phrase groups, and built an aggressive shared negative library against consumer terms.

    SKAG HybridNegative ListsIntent Mapping

  3. Product-specific landing pages

    Built dedicated pages per product line with matched headlines, sector proof, and a shortened form. Message-match went from generic to specific at every entry point.

    Landing Page CROA/B Testing

  4. Performance Max with asset-group discipline

    Deployed PMax only after value tracking was trustworthy, with audience signals drawn from closed-won customer lists and asset groups split by service.

    Google Ads PMaxCustomer Match
