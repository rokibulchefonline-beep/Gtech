Overview

About the Project

ChefOnline is one of the UK's largest independent online food ordering marketplaces, connecting diners with over 1,800 restaurants nationwide. GTech Digital has acted as the platform's growth and engineering partner since 2019, owning both the organic acquisition strategy and the ordering infrastructure that fulfils it.

The engagement spans two tightly coupled workstreams: an enterprise SEO programme that had to scale location pages across thousands of postcodes without triggering thin-content penalties, and a re-architecture of the ordering stack so it could absorb Friday and Saturday evening traffic spikes without dropping baskets.

The Challenge

What Was Holding Growth Back

  • Thin-content risk across 1,800+ location pages

    Templated restaurant and postcode pages were near-duplicates. Google was indexing a fraction of them, and the ones that did index cannibalised each other for the same local queries.

  • Checkout collapse at peak throughput

    Between 18:00 and 21:00 on weekends the monolithic PHP ordering flow hit database contention. Basket abandonment spiked and a measurable share of orders failed at the payment step.

  • No local presence in high-value postcodes

    Competitors held the Google Maps 3-Pack in the dense urban areas that drove the highest average order value. ChefOnline was effectively invisible at the exact moment of purchase intent.

  • Fragmented tracking across web and app

    Web and mobile analytics ran on separate, inconsistent schemas, so nobody could attribute revenue to channel with confidence or calculate true customer acquisition cost.

The Solution

How We Solved It

  1. Programmatic SEO with genuine per-location substance

    Rebuilt location pages around real, unique data per area — cuisine density, delivery radius, opening-hour patterns and live menu inventory — so each page earned its index slot instead of repeating a template.

    Programmatic SEOSchema.orgInternal Linking

  2. Decoupled the ordering pipeline

    Moved order intake onto a queue-backed service with Redis write-buffering and read replicas, isolating checkout from catalogue reads. Peak-hour failures stopped being a capacity problem.

    LaravelRedisPostgreSQLQueue Workers

  3. Google Business Profile programme at scale

    Built a managed pipeline for profile completeness, category accuracy, review velocity and geo-tagged posts across every serviced postcode, prioritised by commercial value rather than alphabetically.

    Local SEOGBP ManagementReview Ops

  4. Unified the measurement layer

    Standardised a single server-side event schema across web and React Native, giving one attribution model, one definition of a conversion, and reliable CAC by channel.

    GA4Server-Side TaggingReact Native

  5. Core Web Vitals remediation

    Cut the largest contentful paint on menu pages by deferring non-critical bundles, serving responsive image formats, and moving search to an Elasticsearch index.

    ElasticsearchCWVPerformance
