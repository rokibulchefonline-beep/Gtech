Overview

About the Project

Aura is a late-night lounge and events venue whose audience lives almost entirely on short-form video. Its content was competent but generic — venue footage set to trending audio, indistinguishable from every other venue in the city.

We rebuilt the content approach around retention rather than production value, then put paid budget behind only what the organic data proved.

The Challenge

What Was Holding Growth Back

  • Low retention past three seconds

    Videos opened with establishing shots of the venue. The majority of viewers left before the content reached anything distinctive.

  • No repeatable content formats

    Every post was made from scratch, which capped output and meant nothing compounded — no format ever got the repetition needed to build recognition.

  • Paid social amplifying weak creative

    Budget was spread evenly across posts regardless of organic performance, amplifying content the audience had already declined to watch.

  • Views not converting to bookings

    Reach existed but there was no path from a video to a table booking, so social performance never showed up in revenue.

The Solution

How We Solved It

  1. Retention-first editing standards

    Rebuilt openings around motion, a visible hook and on-screen text within the first second, with pattern interrupts placed at measured drop-off points.

    Hook DesignRetention Editing

  2. Repeatable format library

    Defined a small set of recurring formats — resident DJ sets, bartender craft, event build-up — that could be produced quickly and improved through repetition.

    Content SystemsFormat Design

  3. Data-gated paid amplification

    Put paid budget only behind posts that cleared an organic retention threshold, so spend followed proven creative instead of preceding it.

    TikTok AdsCreative Gating

  4. Creator collaboration programme

    Built a recurring roster of local creators with clear briefs and usage rights, extending reach into adjacent audiences at predictable cost.

    Creator PartnershipsUGC Rights

  5. Booking path from video

    Wired every profile and paid placement to a single-step booking flow with event-specific landing pages, so attention had somewhere to go.

    Landing PagesBooking Funnel
