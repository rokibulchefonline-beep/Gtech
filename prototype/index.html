How this is calculated

Uplift assumptions come from industry benchmarks, set deliberately at the low end. Larger budgets scale on a square-root curve capped at 1.5× to reflect diminishing returns. Month 6 is modelled at 60% of the 12-month uplift, because campaigns ramp rather than switch on.

ROI is calculated on gross profit, not revenue, using a typical contribution margin for your sector. In lead-generation sectors a conversion is an enquiry, so a sector close rate is applied before counting revenue. Above 500% the model is extrapolating, so a floor is shown rather than false precision.
