How this is calculated

Uplift assumptions are drawn from industry benchmarks and set deliberately at the low end. Larger budgets are scaled with a square-root curve to reflect diminishing returns rather than a straight line, and capped at 1.5×. Month 6 is modelled at 60% of the 12-month uplift, because search and paid campaigns ramp rather than switch on.

ROI is calculated on gross profit, not revenue, using a typical contribution margin for your sector. Quoting ROI on revenue is standard practice in agency calculators and overstates the return by roughly two to three times. Above 500% the model is extrapolating past what it can support, so we show a floor instead of a precise number.

In lead-generation sectors a conversion is an enquiry, not a sale, so we apply a sector close rate before counting revenue — 25% for professional services, 20% for B2B SaaS, 30% for trades, 35% for healthcare. E-commerce and hospitality are treated as transactional, where the conversion is the sale.

Nothing here is a lead-capture trick: the numbers move down as readily as up, and the assumptions are on this page so you can argue with them.

This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Actual results depend on your market, competition, offer and starting position. In this prototype the benchmark figures are placeholders and must be replaced with GTech's own campaign data before launch.
